A guide only. Always follow the rating plate and connection diagram from the manufacturer; we check this at your home.

Two-phase or three-phase?

With two-phase (cooking circuit 2x230V) we spread the power over two phases, which is enough for most induction hobs up to 7.4 kW. With three-phase (400V) we spread the load over three phases for heavy hobs and cookers with an oven.
