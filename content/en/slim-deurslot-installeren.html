WHAT IS A RETROFIT LOCK?

Making what you already have smart

A retrofit lock goes on the inside, over your existing cylinder. From the outside your door looks the same. Your key still works as a backup.

Open with your phone

Open the door via the app, even remotely. Handy when a parcel or a cleaner arrives.

Temporary access

Give family or a babysitter access for a day or a fixed time slot, without having extra keys cut.

Automatic locking

Many smart locks lock the door by themselves when you leave. So you never forget again.

Combine with your doorbell

With a video doorbell you can see who is at the door and open it straight away.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

What we pay attention to

1. The right cylinder

Many smart locks need a cylinder with an emergency function, so the key works from the outside even when the lock is in the cylinder on the inside. We measure the size and fit the right cylinder.

2. The door closes smoothly

A smart lock has little power. If the door sticks, the lock won't manage. We check the locking hardware and adjust it where possible.

3. Range and pairing

We link the lock to your phone and wifi or a bridge and test that it works everywhere. Afterwards you know how to add codes and users.
