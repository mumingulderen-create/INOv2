EVERY WALL IS DIFFERENT

The right fixings for your wall

A heavy TV needs the right plug and screw. What works in concrete won't hold in plasterboard. We first check what your wall is made of.

Concrete

Strong, but hard to drill into. Common in new builds and flats. We drill with low dust and take account of cables and pipes in the wall.

Brick and sand-lime brick

Common in older homes. We choose plugs that hold well, even with a bracket that swings out.

Plasterboard (stud wall)

This needs extra care. We find the studs or use special plugs for hollow walls.

Cables and pipes in the wall

Before we drill, we check for power cables and pipes. That way we don't drill through anything.

NEATLY FINISHED

Cable trunking

The quickest solution. Neat trunking in the colour of your wall, which you can paint over later.

Cables in the wall

The neatest option: we chase a channel with low dust and lay a conduit in the wall. Afterwards you plaster or paint the wall. See wall chasing and sockets.
