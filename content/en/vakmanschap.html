MBO 2 Low-Voltage Distribution

BEI LS VP & STIPEL

NEN 3140 VP & NEN 1010

Certified qualified person (NEN 3140 VP) for working safely and expertly on electrical installations. All installation work is completed strictly in line with NEN 1010.

Recognising asbestos in the meter cupboard

SBK Paving & Digging

Certified by SBK Opleidingen for professional groundwork and paving. Neat, invisible laying of underground cables, garden lighting and EV chargers without damaging the driveway.
