LIKE-FOR-LIKE SWAP

How swapping a hob works

From gas or ceramic to induction, or replacing an old induction hob. It comes down to three things: does the hob fit, can your connection handle it, and is everything safely connected.

Does the cut-out fit?

We compare the installation dimensions of your new hob with the hole in your worktop. Send us the dimensions beforehand and we will know whether it fits like for like.

Can your connection handle it?

An induction hob draws a lot of power. We look at the rating plate and your fuse box. See the table on the Perilex and cooking circuit page.

Ventilation under the hob

Induction needs space to cool. If there is a drawer or oven underneath, we check the clearance according to the manufacturer's instructions.

From gas to induction?

We take care of the electrical side. The gas pipe must be capped by a registered gas installer. We tell you this upfront.
