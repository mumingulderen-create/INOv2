FLUSH OR SURFACE-MOUNTED?

When to chase and when not to?

Chasing gives the neatest result, but it is not always the best choice. We give you honest advice on what suits your wall and your planning.

Chasing and flush mounting

The best choice if you are going to plaster, paint or wallpaper anyway. Cables and boxes disappear into the wall. You only see the socket or switch.

Surface-mounted with trunking

Handy if the wall has just been finished, for a concrete wall you don't want to open up, or in a shed, garage or meter cupboard. Quicker and cheaper, but visible.

Via the ceiling or crawl space

Sometimes a cable can run via a suspended ceiling, an empty conduit or the crawl space. Then less or no chasing is needed. We look at this case by case.
