WHICH SERVICE SUITS YOU?

From an old meter cupboard to an all-electric home

The job you need often depends on the age of your home and what you want to connect.

Older home (before 1970)

Often only a few circuits, no or too few RCDs and sometimes wiring without an earth. A new fuse box is usually the first step here. After that you can safely extend.

New kitchen or renovation

An induction hob needs its own cooking circuit with a Perilex socket. The dishwasher, oven and fridge should be on their own circuits. Sockets in a new place? Then we recess them neatly into the wall.

EV charger, heat pump or solar panels

Heavy appliances need their own circuit, sometimes three-phase. Read more about EV charger installation, three-phase power and adding an extra circuit.

Garden, shed or canopy

Stricter rules apply outdoors: underground cable at the right depth, watertight connections and preferably a separate RCD. See garden lighting and outdoor electrics.
