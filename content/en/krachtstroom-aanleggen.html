USES OF THREE-PHASE POWER

What is a 400V three-phase circuit installed for?

Standard 230V sockets deliver a maximum of 3,680 watts. Heavier equipment needs a three-phase circuit (up to 11,000 W or more) for safe use without overloading.

Heat pumps & hybrid systems

Many all-electric and hybrid heat pumps need their own three-phase supply with an isolator switch on the outside so maintenance can be done safely.

Jacuzzi, hot tub & sauna

The electric heaters in hot tubs and saunas draw high power continuously. We install a special waterproof three-phase supply with separate RCD protection.

Shed, summer house & workshop

Want to use heavy machines, a car lift, a welder or a sub-distribution board in your shed? We run a heavy-duty underground cable from the main building.

Large PV installation (solar panels)

From around 16 panels or a heavier three-phase inverter, a three-phase connection in the fuse box is needed to prevent grid congestion and outages.

CRAFTSMANSHIP & CALCULATION

How do we determine the right three-phase installation?

Three-phase power needs precise technical calculations. Every situation is unique:

1. Space and support in the meter cupboard

We first check whether your current fuse box is already connected to three-phase and whether there is physical DIN-rail space for a 4-pole RCBO. If needed, we can extend the fuse box straight away.

2. The cable route & distance

Does the cable need to go through the crawl space, along the outside wall, via the attic or through the garden? The distance and route determine which type of cable (for example YMvK or XMvK-as underground cable) is needed.

3. Cable size to prevent voltage loss

For longer distances (for example to a summer house or the back garden) we calculate exactly which conductor size is needed (for example 2.5 mm², 4 mm² or 6 mm²) so the voltage does not drop under load.

4. Characteristic & type of socket

Appliances with a high inrush current (such as compressors or heavy pumps) need a C characteristic instead of the standard B characteristic, plus a suitable red CEE socket (16A or 32A).

Take a clear photo of your open fuse box and a photo or short video of where the three-phase circuit or socket needs to go. That way we can analyse the situation straight away and you quickly receive a fixed price upfront!
