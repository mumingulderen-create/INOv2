A PRICE FASTER

This helps us give you a price quickly

With a few photos we can often tell you straight away what a job costs. Send these via WhatsApp or the form:

  • ✓ A photo of your open meter cupboard (circuits and main switch clearly visible)
  • ✓ A photo of the place where the work needs to be done, for example the kitchen or the wall
  • ✓ Your postcode, so we know whether there are travel costs
  • ✓ For a fault: what you were doing when it went wrong and which circuit or switch tripped
