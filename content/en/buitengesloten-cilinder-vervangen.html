WHEN SHOULD YOU CALL US?

Common situations

Not every lockout is the same. Tell us on the phone what has happened and you will know upfront what we are going to do.

Door slammed shut

The door is closed but not locked with the key. This is often the quickest situation and the cylinder usually stays intact.

Door locked, key lost

If the door has been locked with the key, the cylinder often has to come out. We fit a new cylinder with new keys straight away.

Key snapped in the lock

We remove the broken piece of key. If the cylinder is damaged, we replace it straight away.

Keys stolen or lost

Lost your keys outside the house? Then a new cylinder is a sensible choice. That way nobody can get in with your old key.

A fair price, no surprises afterwards

When people are locked out, they are often caught out by inflated prices from national middlemen. INO is a local Utrecht company registered with the Chamber of Commerce (KvK) and with a fixed workshop. You call the person who actually comes out, and you hear the price before we start.

LOCK CYLINDER REPLACEMENT

A new cylinder without being locked out

Just moved, changed tenants or is the lock stiff? We replace your cylinder by appointment. All doors in the house on one key is also possible: this is called a keyed-alike set.

The right size

We measure the cylinder. A cylinder that sticks out too far is easier to break off. The new cylinder fits your door furniture exactly.

A choice of security levels

Want extra burglary resistance? You can choose a cylinder with the SKG quality mark. Ask about the options.

Adding a smart lock?

Thinking about a smart lock? Then we fit a suitable cylinder straight away. See smart lock installation.
