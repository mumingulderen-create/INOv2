Every neighbourhood has its own typical jobs

The age of the homes often determines what work is needed. That is why each neighbourhood below has its own page with the housing types, the local areas and the jobs we do there most often, plus answers to questions from residents.

Old city centre and pre-war neighbourhoods

In the city centre, East and West and Lombok we often find old wiring and small meter cupboards. Much of the work revolves around a new fuse box and extending safely.

Post-war neighbourhoods

In Overvecht, Kanaleneiland, Transwijk, Hoograven and Nieuwegein there are many flats and family homes from the 1950s to the 1980s, often still with the original fuse box. Think of too few circuits for a new kitchen and a Perilex for induction.

New-build areas and growth towns

In Leidsche Rijn, Vleuten-De Meern and Houten the work is more often an EV charger, three-phase power for a heat pump or garden lighting.
