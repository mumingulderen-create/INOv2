DIPLOMAS & QUALIFICATIONS

Proven expertise and safety

Officially qualified and certified in line with current quality and safety requirements.

Official accreditation

SBB accredited training company

Officially accredited by SBB, the Dutch Foundation for Cooperation on Vocational Education, Training and the Labour Market (SBB relation number: 100850431). INO trains the next generation of electricians on the job.

MBO 2 Low-Voltage Distribution

State-recognised diploma as a Low-Voltage Distribution Electrician (Crebo 25769 / Techniek College Rotterdam) with the optional module Smart Meters. A solid electrical foundation (currently training for medium voltage).

BEI LS VP & STIPEL

Appointed low-voltage qualified person (BEI LS VP / STIPEL VP LS networks). Officially certified to work safely on power supply systems and low-voltage networks.

NEN 3140 VP & NEN 1010

Certified qualified person (NEN 3140 VP) for working safely and expertly on electrical installations. All installation work is completed strictly in line with NEN 1010.

Recognising asbestos in the meter cupboard

Certified via &fonds NViB (Basic & Module 4: Asbestos in the meter cupboard). Essential for safe renovations and fuse box replacements in older homes in Utrecht.

SBK Paving & Digging

Certified by SBK Opleidingen for professional groundwork and paving. Neat, invisible laying of underground cables, garden lighting and EV chargers without damaging the driveway.

Safety: VCA VOL & BHV

Safety for Operational Supervisors (VCA VOL) and certified company emergency responder (BHV). Extensive infrastructure experience gained with grid operators and contractors (including Stedin, Liander and Heijmans).
