Ventilation unit (MV box) replacement in Utrecht

Is your ventilation unit very noisy, not extracting well or broken? We replace your mechanical ventilation with a new, energy-efficient unit. We connect it to your existing ducts and pair the controls.

WHEN TO REPLACE?

Signs that your ventilation unit needs replacing

A lot of noise

Humming, rattling or squeaking? That is often worn bearings. A new unit is usually much quieter.

Poor extraction

Misted-up windows, a damp bathroom or cooking smells that linger. Then your unit is not extracting enough.

High power consumption

Old AC units use much more electricity than a modern DC unit. It runs all day, so you notice the difference.

Unit no longer works

Does the unit cut out or not respond to the switch? We first check whether the problem is the unit or the power supply.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

What we pay attention to

1. DC or AC

A DC (direct current) unit is more efficient and quieter. We advise which type suits your home and your ducts.

2. Matching connections

We measure the duct diameters and the position of the connections. That way the new unit connects airtight to your existing ducts.

3. Connection and controls

Older units often have a Perilex plug for the speed switch. We connect the new unit properly and pair a wireless (RF) control if you want one.

What does it cost?

Replacement is charged at the hourly rate: € 90 for the first hour during the day on weekdays, then per quarter hour. The new unit and controls are charged separately; you hear the price upfront. Within the municipality of Utrecht the call-out charge is € 0. Outside it we charge a fixed € 0.40 per km. Feel free to send a photo via WhatsApp first; that is free. Prefer us to come and look? A site visit costs € 90, which we deduct in full if you go ahead.
