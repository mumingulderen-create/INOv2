WHAT SHOULD YOU CHOOSE?

Appointment, quote or emergency?

Not every question needs a visit. This is how you choose the fastest route:

Site visit

Useful for a larger job or when photos don't tell the whole story, such as an old meter cupboard, a renovation or an EV charger with a long cable route. Costs € 90, fully deducted if you give us the job.

Quote based on photos

For most standard jobs a photo is enough, for example a Perilex connection or an extra circuit. That is free. Go to the quote form →

Power fault

No power or a circuit that keeps tripping? Don't use this form, call us directly: 06 28 76 37 75. We are available 24/7. See emergency electrician Utrecht.

HOW IT WORKS

What happens during a site visit?

01

We confirm the appointment

You choose a preferred day and time slot. We call or WhatsApp you to agree the final time.

02

We look at the situation

We look at your meter cupboard, the cabling and the place where the work is needed. That way we see what is needed and what is possible, for example whether there is room for extra circuits.

03

You get a fixed price

After the site visit you receive a clear price upfront, including VAT. If you agree, we deduct the € 90 for the site visit from the total.

Would you rather look at the prices first? See our prices or the overview of all services. Within the municipality of Utrecht the call-out charge is € 0. Outside it we charge a fixed € 0.40 per km.
