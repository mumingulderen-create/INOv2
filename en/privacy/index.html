Privacy statement

Brief and clear: which data we use, what for and for how long. This is a translation; the Dutch version is the official one.

Who are we?

INO Techniek en Installatie, based in Utrecht. Questions about privacy? Email info@ino-elektra.nl or call 06 28 76 37 75.

Which data do we use?

Only what you give us yourself: name, phone number, email address, the address of the job, your description and any photos. We use this data solely to answer your request, carry out the job and send the invoice.

Forms

When you send a form, your request goes over a secure connection to our own processing service at Cloudflare. It checks the request and forwards it via the email service Brevo to our mailbox. If you entered an email address, you receive a short confirmation from us by email. Your request and photos are not stored along the way; they only end up in our mailbox. We automatically remove location data (GPS) from photos.

To prevent spam and abuse we use Cloudflare Turnstile. It checks that a human is filling in the form, without advertising or tracking cookies. To limit abuse, your IP address is briefly used to count the number of submissions per minute; we do not store it.

WhatsApp messages go through WhatsApp (Meta). We never sell or share your data with other parties for marketing.

How long do we keep your data?

Requests that do not lead to a job are deleted within 12 months. We keep invoices and related data for 7 years, because Dutch law (the tax retention obligation) requires this.

Cookies and consent

On your first visit the cookie notice asks whether you allow us two things. Under "Preferences" you choose them separately. Nothing is switched on in advance, and "Necessary only" is just as quick as "Accept all".

  • Statistics (Google Analytics 4): which pages are viewed and how often call, WhatsApp or form buttons are used. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics.
  • Ad measurement (Google Ads): whether a click on our Google advert leads to a call, WhatsApp message or request. For this, Google receives data about your visit and the ad click. We do not use remarketing or personalised advertising.

Without consent we load no Google scripts, place no Google cookies and send nothing to Google. If you do consent, we pass on your choice through Google Consent Mode v2; consent for personalised advertising is always off. Google (Google Ireland Limited) may also process data in the United States. We do not sell data. The font is hosted on our own server, so nothing is sent to Google for it.

Your choice is valid for 12 months, after which we ask again. You can change or withdraw it at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, measuring stops straight away and we remove the Google cookies for ino-elektra.nl from your browser.

Your rights

You can always ask which data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Send us an email and we will arrange it within 30 days. If you disagree with something, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
