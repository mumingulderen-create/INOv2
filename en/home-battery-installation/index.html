SAFETY IN THE METER CUPBOARD

Why the electrical connection is crucial

A home battery can continuously deliver and draw high currents. A poor connection can lead to overheating or a short circuit. We make sure the installation meets the strictest standards.

  • Its own dedicated RCBO: A home battery must never simply be added behind any existing RCD. We fit a separate RCBO with the right tripping characteristic.
  • External isolator switch: In line with NEN 1010 we fit a clearly marked isolator switch within sight of the battery. That way the system can be made dead in one action for maintenance or emergencies.
  • Correct cable size: We calculate the right cable size (2.5 mm², 4 mm² or 6 mm² YMvK) to prevent voltage loss and heat build-up.
  • Current transformers (CTs) & P1 link: Accurate fitting of measuring coils in the fuse box so the system knows exactly when there is surplus power or power is being bought in.
  • Completion test and earth check: We measure the earth loop impedance and the disconnection time of the protective devices straight after installation.

Do you have a single-phase or three-phase connection?

Small home batteries (up to about 3.6 kW) can be installed on a single-phase connection. Larger batteries or systems with high charge and discharge rates need a three-phase connection to prevent overloading the main fuse.

Does your meter cupboard still have a single-phase main switch? We can make your fuse box ready for three-phase straight away, and for an EV charger or induction hob too.
