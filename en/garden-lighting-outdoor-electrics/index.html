OUTDOOR ELECTRICAL SERVICES

From a subtle ground spotlight to a complete garden installation

Outdoor electrics must withstand rain, frost and ground moisture. We make sure you have an installation you can enjoy worry-free for decades.

Mood lighting & ground spotlights

Garden posts, wall lights on fences or recessed ground spotlights along the garden path and patio. Switchable from inside, with an app or via an automatic dusk sensor.

Splash-proof outdoor sockets (IP55/IP66)

Handy power points on the patio, by the barbecue or in the lawn for the lawnmower. Neatly and safely fitted against moisture and the weather.

Canopy, veranda & summer house

Complete electrics for your veranda or summer house: recessed ceiling spotlights, heaters, heater switches, sockets and, if needed, a separate sub-distribution board.

Pond pumps, fountains & irrigation

A reliable and safe power supply for pond filters, UV lamps and automated irrigation systems with separate RCD protection.

CRAFTSMANSHIP & TAILORED QUOTE

Why a tailored quote for outdoor electrics?

For garden projects, fixed standard prices are not fair, because every garden is laid out differently:

Soil type & digging

Sandy soil is easier to dig than heavy clay or compacted rubble under the driveway. We assess upfront what is needed for the required depth of 50–60 cm.

Professional paving work

Does the cable need to go under a neatly laid patio or brick paving? We work closely with skilled fellow paviors so your paving is relaid perfectly straight and seamless.

Cable size & voltage loss

In long gardens (20 to 50+ metres) voltage drops if the cable is too thin. We calculate the exact cable size so lights don't flicker and pumps run at full power.

A separate garden RCD

We prefer to connect outdoor electrics to a separate RCD in the fuse box. That way moisture in a garden light won't immediately cut the power to the fridge in the house!

HOW WE GO ABOUT IT

From plan to working garden lighting

01

Plan and price

Send a sketch or photos of your garden and show where lights and sockets need to go. Is the garden large or complicated? Then we come and look during a site visit for € 90, deducted if you go ahead.

02

Circuit and cable

We connect the outdoor electrics to their own circuit in the meter cupboard, preferably with separate RCD protection. The underground cable is laid deep enough so you can safely dig later.

03

Connection and testing

We fit lights, switches and sockets, make the joints watertight and test everything. We explain how it all works, for example a dusk sensor or timer.

COSTS

What does installing garden lighting cost?

Every garden is different, so we work with a tailored price here. These factors determine the price:

  • ✓ The length of the cable route from the meter cupboard to the garden
  • ✓ Whether the cable needs to go under paving, a patio or a driveway
  • ✓ The number of lights, sockets and switches
  • ✓ Whether there is still room in the meter cupboard for an extra circuit

No free space in your meter cupboard? Then an extra circuit is added, from € 60 in an existing fuse box. If the fuse box is full or old, a new fuse box is sometimes the better choice. Hourly work costs € 90 per hour during the day on weekdays. All prices: see our prices page.
