FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about fuse boxes

Replacing a single-phase fuse box (up to 8 circuits) starts from € 695 all-in. A three-phase version (up to 8 circuits) starts from € 815 all-in. This includes reliable components (Hager, ABB or Schneider), professional installation, VAT and environmentally friendly disposal of the old materials. For units with more circuits or specific extras, we give you a fixed price upfront based on photos or a site visit.
The meter cupboard (meterkast) is the recess or small cupboard that holds the energy meter and the grid operator's main connection box (for example Stedin). The fuse box is the distribution board with switches and circuit breakers that distributes power throughout your home. That is the part INO replaces and extends.
For flats and average family homes with gas heating, a single-phase unit is usually enough. As soon as you switch to full-power electric cooking or get an EV charger or heat pump, a three-phase connection is needed to prevent overloading.
Certainly. Combining extras while the fuse box is being replaced saves a lot compared with a separate visit later, because the board is already open and switched off.
For a standard family home, 6 to 8 circuits is common. If you have several heavy appliances such as a dishwasher, oven, dryer and induction hob, we often recommend 8 to 12 circuits. Send a photo of your current situation for tailored advice.
You get a full 12-month installation guarantee on the work and up to 2 years' manufacturer's warranty on the premium-brand parts fitted, in line with NEN 1010 guidelines.
