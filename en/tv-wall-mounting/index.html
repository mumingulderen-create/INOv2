TV wall mounting in Utrecht

A large TV belongs firmly and straight on the wall. We choose the right wall plugs for your wall, hang the TV at the right height and hide the cables neatly. We can also sort out a socket behind the TV at the same time.

EVERY WALL IS DIFFERENT

The right fixings for your wall

A heavy TV needs the right plug and screw. What works in concrete won't hold in plasterboard. We first check what your wall is made of.

Concrete

Strong, but hard to drill into. Common in new builds and flats. We drill with low dust and take account of cables and pipes in the wall.

Brick and sand-lime brick

Common in older homes. We choose plugs that hold well, even with a bracket that swings out.

Plasterboard (stud wall)

This needs extra care. We find the studs or use special plugs for hollow walls.

Cables and pipes in the wall

Before we drill, we check for power cables and pipes. That way we don't drill through anything.

NEATLY FINISHED

No cables in sight

Cable trunking

The quickest solution. Neat trunking in the colour of your wall, which you can paint over later.

Cables in the wall

The neatest option: we chase a channel with low dust and lay a conduit in the wall. Afterwards you plaster or paint the wall. See wall chasing and sockets.

Socket behind the TV

We move a socket to behind the TV, from € 120 per point. Then you no longer see any plugs.

What does it cost?

Mounting is charged at the hourly rate: € 90 for the first hour during the day on weekdays, then per quarter hour. You bring the bracket yourself or we agree it beforehand. Wall chasing costs € 10 per metre in plasterboard, € 15 in brick and € 30 in concrete. Filling in the chase is not included. Within the municipality of Utrecht the call-out charge is € 0. Outside it we charge a fixed € 0.40 per km. Feel free to send a photo via WhatsApp first; that is free. Prefer us to come and look? A site visit costs € 90, which we deduct in full if you go ahead.
