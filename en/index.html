Power cut: call Stedin or INO?

🏛️ Grid operator Stedin

When do you call Stedin?

For faults in the neighbourhood power grid or at the sealed main connection.

Typical signs:

  • ✕ The whole street or your neighbours have no power either
  • ✕ Street lights outside are off or flickering
  • ✕ The electricity meter display is completely blank
  • ✕ The sealed main fuse below the meter has blown
Action: contact the grid operator 📞 Call Stedin: 0800 9009 Official outage procedure on Stedin.net (Dutch) ↗
⚡ INO electricians (24/7 emergency)

When do you call INO?

Fixed all-in rate: € 90 (08:00–18:00) · € 120 (18:00–22:00) · € 145 (night & weekend)

Typical signs:

  • ✓ Only your home is dark (the neighbours have power)
  • ✓ The RCD (the switch with the 'T' button) keeps tripping
  • ✓ A circuit breaker trips again as soon as you switch it on
  • ✓ Crackling or a burning smell at a socket or the fuse box
Action: call our emergency service 📞 Call INO now: 06 28 76 37 75 ⚡ Emergency in Utrecht: within 5–30 min, region 15–40 min
