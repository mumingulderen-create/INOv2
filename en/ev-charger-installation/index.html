CRAFTSMANSHIP & TECHNOLOGY

How do we handle the electrical installation?

An EV charger draws high power continuously. A safe installation starts in the fuse box and only ends at the plug.

Its own RCBO in the fuse box

An EV charger must never be placed on a shared circuit. We always fit a separate, suitable RCBO (B16, B20 or B32 depending on the charger type) in line with NEN 1010.

Correct cable sizing

No risk of overheating or voltage drop over long distances. We calculate the exact conductor size needed: from 3x2.5 mm² or 5x2.5 mm² up to 5x4 mm² or 5x6 mm² YMvK(-as) underground cable for higher power ratings.

Cable route: neat and invisible

Where possible we run the cable neatly through the crawl space. Does the work need to go through the garden? We handle the digging and paving to the driveway. Indoors we finish with neat conduit, a tidy boxing-in or fully chased into the plaster.

Dynamic load balancing

With an energy module or current clamps in the meter cupboard, the charger takes your total power use into account in real time. When the induction hob, oven and washing machine are running at the same time, the charger automatically scales back to prevent the main fuse from tripping.

Single-phase now? Ready for three-phase

Do you still have single-phase from Stedin but want to charge at full power (11 kW) later? We build the fuse box and cabling ready for three-phase straight away. As soon as Stedin comes to upgrade your connection, you switch over without extra cabling work.

Testing, inspection & handover

After installation we test the earth resistance, RCDs and communication with the car using official installation testers. You get a full 12-month installation guarantee.
