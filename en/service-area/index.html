Service area: electrician in Utrecht and the surrounding area

INO works for homeowners, tenants and businesses in the city and province of Utrecht, from Woerden to Amersfoort and from Breukelen to Vianen.

Service area of INO Techniek en Installatie: working throughout Utrecht and the surrounding area. We set off from Overvecht.

RESPONSE TIME AND CALL-OUT CHARGES

What do you pay and how quickly are we there?

Because we set off from Overvecht, we are quick to reach anywhere in the city. For the region we only charge a fixed rate per kilometre.

AreaCall-out chargeResponse time for a fault
Municipality of Utrecht (all neighbourhoods, including Leidsche Rijn and Vleuten-De Meern)€ 0usually 5–30 minutes
Region (including Nieuwegein, Maarssen, Houten, Zeist, IJsselstein, Vianen, Breukelen)€ 0.40 per kmusually 15–40 minutes

The response time is a guide and depends on traffic and other emergency calls. When you call, you hear on the phone what time we will arrive. You can find the hourly rates per time slot on our prices page.
