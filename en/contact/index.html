AVAILABILITY

When and how quickly we respond

Power fault or emergency

Day and night, including weekends. Call 06 28 76 37 75. Within the municipality of Utrecht we are usually with you within 5–30 minutes, and within 15–40 minutes in the region. See emergency electrician for the rates.

Quote or planned job

Send your request via the quote form or WhatsApp. Would you like us to take a look first? Book a site visit for € 90, deducted if you go ahead.

Question about a price

Our hourly rates and fixed prices are on the prices page. During the day on weekdays you pay € 90 per hour, incl. VAT.

A PRICE FASTER

This helps us give you a price quickly

With a few photos we can often tell you straight away what a job costs. Send these via WhatsApp or the form:

  • ✓ A photo of your open meter cupboard (circuits and main switch clearly visible)
  • ✓ A photo of the place where the work needs to be done, for example the kitchen or the wall
  • ✓ Your postcode, so we know whether there are travel costs
  • ✓ For a fault: what you were doing when it went wrong and which circuit or switch tripped

Within the municipality of Utrecht the call-out charge is € 0. Outside it we charge a fixed € 0.40 per km.
