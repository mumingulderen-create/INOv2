CRAFTSMANSHIP AS AGREED

Clear agreements, professional installation and a clean finish

No surprises afterwards. With INO Techniek you know exactly where you stand upfront: from the first photo via WhatsApp to a safe, tested installation.

What can you expect?Here we explain step by step how we handle planned projects and urgent faults in Utrecht, how we carry out measurements and how we leave your home clean and tidy.
OUR APPROACH IN 4 STEPS

From first photo to safe completion

This is how an electrical project with INO Techniek works in practice.

01

Photo via WhatsApp or online

Send a photo of your fuse box or situation via WhatsApp or the quote form. In 90% of cases we can assess the technical situation straight away and quickly send you a clear estimate.

02

Fixed price & booking a date

You receive a clear all-in quote including premium-brand materials, installation and VAT. A deal is a deal: if something unexpected turns up on site, we always discuss it with you first. We only continue after you agree.

03

Safe installation & dust extraction

We work in line with NEN 1010 and NEN 3140, using a professional construction vacuum cleaner, brush and dustpan. After the job we leave everything clean and dispose of old materials separately.

04

Testing, checking & guarantee

We thoroughly test the installation with calibrated measuring equipment (RCD trip time, earthing, insulation resistance). The fuse box is neatly labelled, with a full 12-month guarantee.

TWO SITUATIONS

Planned project or urgent fault?

We adapt our approach straight away to your request.

Planned project

Fuse box, cooking circuit or EV charger

For planned changes and upgrades to the electrical installation.

  • Quote upfront: A no-obligation price based on photos or a targeted site visit.
  • Fixed date: Together we agree a day and time that suits you.
  • Switching off only what is needed: If the fuse box needs replacing, the main switch is off for about 2 to 3 hours. For extensions we only switch off the specific circuit; the rest of your home keeps its power.

Urgent fault

24/7 emergency service for power cuts

For RCD faults, short circuits or a sudden loss of power in Utrecht.

  • Phone triage: Direct contact with the electrician to check whether you can safely switch it back on yourself or whether help is needed straight away.
  • Fast response: Usually on site within 5–30 minutes in the municipality of Utrecht and within 15–40 minutes in the region.
  • Systematic testing: We isolate the fault with professional measuring equipment and safely restore the power supply.

CERTAINTY & GUARANTEE

Our 5 fixed promises

You can rely on these with every job.

1. A deal is a deal

100% transparent. A clear price upfront, no unexpected extra costs and always an honest conversation before anything changes.

2. Premium-brand materials only

We only install certified premium brands such as ABB Haf, Hager, Schneider Electric and Attema. No cheap second-rate components.

3. NEN 1010 & NEN 3140

Every connection is installed in line with the current Dutch safety standards and thoroughly tested before it is put into use.

4. Clean & sorted waste

We bring a professional construction vacuum cleaner, tidy everything up and dispose of old fuse boxes and leftover materials (copper, plastic, cardboard) separately.

5. 12-month guarantee & invoice

A clear invoice by email (pay by bank transfer or easily via Tikkie / iDEAL) with a full 12-month installation guarantee.

Direct personal contact
