WHY AN ELECTRICIAN?

A video doorbell is more than just mounting it

A wired video doorbell gets its power through the bell wire and the bell transformer in the meter cupboard. If that transformer is too weak or too old, the doorbell works poorly or keeps restarting.

Checking the bell transformer

We measure the voltage and check whether the transformer suits your doorbell. If it is too light, we fit a new one.

Existing chime

Want to keep your old chime? We check whether that works with your doorbell, or whether a separate chime or adapter is needed.

No bell wire?

If there is no wire at the door, a new cable may be needed. Or you choose a battery-powered doorbell. We advise what suits you best.

Wifi at the front door

A video doorbell needs good wifi. We test the signal at the door and tell you honestly if it is too weak.

What does it cost?

Installation is charged at the hourly rate: € 90 for the first hour during the day on weekdays, then per quarter hour. A new bell transformer is charged separately. Replacing your fuse box as well? Then you can include an integrated bell transformer for € 49. Within the municipality of Utrecht the call-out charge is € 0. Outside it we charge a fixed € 0.40 per km. Feel free to send a photo via WhatsApp first; that is free. Prefer us to come and look? A site visit costs € 90, which we deduct in full if you go ahead.
