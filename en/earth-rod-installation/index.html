Earthing · Earth rod

Earth rod installation in Utrecht

Good earthing makes sure your RCD switches off in time if there is a fault. We drive a new earth rod at your home, connect it in the meter cupboard and test it. You always receive a test report.

WHEN IS IT NEEDED?

When do you need a new earth rod?

EV charger or heat pump

With an EV charger or heat pump we always look at the earthing. If it is not good enough, an earth rod is often the solution.

Solar panels or home battery

An inverter or home battery needs reliable earthing, so leakage currents can drain away safely.

New water pipe

In the past the metal water pipe was often the earth. Has it been replaced with plastic? Then your home may suddenly no longer be properly earthed.

Failed an inspection

During an inspection or a house sale, the earthing value turns out to be too high. In that case an RCD may not switch off in time.

HOW WE WORK

How we install your earth rod

1. Measuring first and KLIC check

We first measure your current earthing. Sometimes an earth rod is not needed. If it is, we make a KLIC notification beforehand (the Dutch underground cable and pipe check). That way we know where cables and pipes are in the ground.

2. Driving the earth rod

Together we choose a spot close to the meter cupboard. Usually one paving tile or a small hole is enough. We drive the rod as deep as needed for a good value.

3. Connecting and testing

We connect the earth rod to the main earthing terminal in the meter cupboard. Then we measure the earth resistance with calibrated measuring equipment.

4. Handover with a test report

We neatly finish the tile or the hole. You receive a test report with the measured value, for your own records or your insurer.

What does it cost?

How deep the rod needs to go depends on the soil. That is why you receive a tailored quote upfront. Feel free to send a photo of your meter cupboard and the spot outside via WhatsApp first; that is free. Prefer us to come and look? A site visit costs € 90, which we deduct in full if you go ahead. Within the municipality of Utrecht the call-out charge is € 0. Outside it we charge a fixed € 0.40 per km.
